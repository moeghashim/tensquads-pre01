Squadmates

One character for the master and for each agent. The body takes the agent's colour from the design. The accessory shows the role. The eyes show what the agent is doing now, from the Agent loop states in the plan. The product adds them next.

Decided by Moe on 3 November 2026
  1. Each squadmate is named after a famous place: Petra, Pisa, Kyoto, Rio, Zurich, Giza, Machu, Angkor and Delphi.
  2. A customer cannot rename the master.
  3. The style stays: one round body, one accessory, eyes that change with the state.

The squad

PetraMaster

Named after Petra, Jordan

Keeps the squad in sync and asks you only when it matters.

PisaOrders

Named after Pisa, Italy

Fulfils orders and chases tracking numbers.
