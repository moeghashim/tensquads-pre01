Agent-UI principles

Agent-UI is the visual language for a node-based agent builder: a quiet neutral chrome, one near-black primary, and six soft tints that tell node families apart. The interface stays out of the way so the graph reads; colour appears only where it carries meaning.

Principles

  • Neutral first. Every surface is a grey; hue is reserved for node families and status. The primary action is near-black (primary-solid), never blue.
  • Layers by lightness, not by lines. Panels float on the canvas as slightly lighter cards with a 0.5px panel-border; nodes are white with a 1px ring (shadow-node). Borders are hairlines from the alpha-* ink scale, never solid grey lines.
  • Soft, then quiet. Fills are 8% ink (input-bg, primary-soft); hovers step to 12%, presses to 16%. Nothing glows.
  • Round where you hold it. Panels and node cards use radius-2xl (16px); tiles, inputs and rows radius-md (8px); anything in the header or floating over the canvas is a pill (radius-full).

Content

Write in sentence case throughout: File search, User approval, Include chat history. Node kinds are nouns; branch labels are single words (Store, Pass, Else). Panel descriptions are one imperative line in the second person: "Call the model with your instructions and tools". Buttons are verbs or nouns of the object they open (Code, Evaluate); never "Click here". Identifiers keep their code form (response_schema, gpt-5-nano). No emoji, no exclamation marks, no trailing periods on labels.

Colour

Set the ground in canvas (gray-75) and read body copy in text on it. Panels (panel) and node cards (node-surface) sit on the canvas; use text-emphasis for names and labels, text-secondary for badges and secondary actions, text-tertiary for section labels, node type lines, placeholders and branch labels.

Node families take their tints from the hue scales at a 25–50 step and never from a solid: tint-agent (Core agents), tint-logic (Classify, If/else, While, User approval), tint-end (Start, End), tint-note, tint-tool (File search, Guardrails, MCP), tint-data (Transform, Set state). The glyph on every tint is node-ink. In dark the tints move to the 900 steps of the same hues (derived, not from the source) so the glyph, now white, still reads at 9:1 or better.

Status uses the semantic pairs: info-* (blue), success-* (green), warning-* (orange), caution-* (yellow), danger-* (red), discovery-* (purple). A status always has a surface pair (*-surface fill with *-surface-text) and a text-on-canvas token (*-text). Solid status fills (info-solid, success-solid) carry white text at under 4.5:1 in the source; use them for icons and 24px+ text only, and prefer the surface pairs for anything smaller. pink-* is in the scale but unused; keep it unused.

Every alpha-NN token is ink at that percentage — #0d0d0d in light, white in dark — so one token works on both themes. Prefer them to literal greys for borders and hovers.

Type

One family, sans, at four weights. The source's face is proprietary and is not shipped; the system names Inter (Google Fonts) as the substitute, with the same sizes, leading and negative tracking (−0.01em body, −0.02em at 24px and up; the builder's own page-title runs at 36/40, weight 590, +0.36px). Code and identifiers use mono (JetBrains Mono).

Use the heading-* and text-* styles for documents and dialogs, and the Product UI styles for the builder chrome: page-title for the workflow name, panel-title and panel-description at the top of the inspector, field-label and field-value for its rows, palette-section and palette-item in the palette, node-title and node-meta on cards, branch-label on branch rows, badge for the Draft pill, button-md for every button and chip. Body text is 16/24; nothing in the chrome is set below 10px (node-meta).

Spacing and sizing

The grid is 4px (space-1). Controls come in nine heights (control-3xs 22px through control-3xl 48px); the builder uses four: 22px badges, 24px palette tiles, 28px inputs, chips and node tiles, 32px buttons, and 40px palette rows and toolbar buttons. Panels are fixed-width: palette-width 200px, inspector-width 360px; the header is header-height 54px. Node cards pad 10px on three sides and 16px on the right (space-2-5, space-4) so the output port has room.

Radii, shadows, motion

radius-2xl panels and nodes; radius-md tiles, inputs, rows, tooltips; radius-sm textareas; radius-full pills, chips, switches, the toolbar. Shadows are rings and lifts, not glows: shadow-node and shadow-node-selected are 1px rings; shadow-toolbar (8px blur at 12%) floats the toolbar; shadow-overlay plus shadow-hairline float tooltips and menus. Motion is short: duration-basic (150ms) with ease-basic for colour, ease-move for the segmented thumb and switch, ease-enter/ease-exit for panels and menus.

Focus and states

Every interactive element shows a 2px ring outline offset 2px on keyboard focus (4.9:1 on canvas in light, 5.0:1 in dark). Hover is a fill change only; press deepens it; selection on the canvas is a darker ring, never a fill. Disabled controls use alpha-05 fill and text-disabled.

Themes and shortcuts

Both themes ship in every token; data-theme on <html> picks one, the ThemeToggle flips it and remembers the choice, and the OS setting decides on first load. Keyboard is first-class: ⌘K opens the CommandPalette (add a node, jump to one, run an action), V/S switch pan and select, ⌘Z/⇧⌘Z undo and redo, ⌘D duplicates, ⌘⇧L tidies the layout, ⌘⇧P previews, ⌘⇧T toggles the theme. Every shortcut is shown as a kbd hint where its action appears.

Chat

Play mode talks to the workflow through the Chat component: the user's words in a primary-soft pill on the right, and on the left one plain row for each step the run touched, with the reply under the agent that produced it. Nothing the model says sits in a bubble — the pill marks the human, the glyph marks the machine. The composer is a rounded surface card pinned to the bottom; its send pill becomes a stop pill while a run is busy, so the one control is always the right one.

Iconography

Line icons on a 16px grid, 1.5px stroke, round caps, currentColor; 16px inside buttons and 28px tiles, 14px inside 24px palette tiles. The set ships in components/bundle.js (AgentUI.icons) as original generic glyphs — the reference product's icon artwork was not copied. Each node kind has exactly one glyph; toolbar and inspector actions use the shared set (pencil, play, hand, cursor, undo, redo, code, plus, more, copy, trash, schema, external, wand, chevron, updown, back, send, attach). No emoji anywhere.

Layout of the builder

The canvas fills the viewport on canvas with an optional dot-grid. The header sits directly on the canvas (no bar). The palette floats at top-left and the inspector at top-right, each page-gutter (8px) from the edge and top-aligned with each other; the toolbar floats bottom-centre. Panels never push the canvas; they cover it.

Components

Each preview is live. It loads agent-ui/tokens.css, agent-ui/components/bundle.css and agent-ui/components/bundle.js. The builders are on window.AgentUI. Their types are in agent-ui/components/index.d.ts.

Actions

Badge

Status pill (Draft) and tone variants

Open

A badge names a state in 12px/600 with 0.36px tracking, 22px tall (control-3xs), radius-full. Default is primary-soft with text-secondary — the Draft badge beside the workflow name, which also carries a 12px up/down glyph when it opens a version menu.

Tones use the status surfaces: success-surface/success-surface-text, warning-…, danger-…, info-…. Every tone also says its state in words; never colour alone.

Do: sentence case. Don't: make badges clickable unless they open a menu (then show the glyph).

Button

Solid, soft, outline, ghost, danger · pill · icon

Open

Buttons are 32px tall by default (control-md), 500 weight, 14px label with a 16px icon at space-1 gap; horizontal padding space-4. Header and toolbar buttons are pills (radius-full); everything else is radius-lg.

Variants: solid (primary-solid, text-inverse) for the one primary action on a screen — the Code button; soft (primary-soft) for secondary actions; outline (1px primary-outline-border) for tertiary; ghost for icon buttons and inline actions, hovering to ghost-hover; danger (danger-solid, white) for destructive confirms only. Disabled is alpha-05 fill with text-disabled. Sizes: sm 28px, md 32px, lg 36px.

The consumer provides label and/or icon and an aria-label on icon-only buttons.

Do: one solid button per surface. Put the icon before the label.
Don't: use a coloured (blue) primary — primary is near-black in this system. Don't use outline and soft side by side for the same rank.

Chip

Attached object (schema, tool) as a removable pill

Open

A chip shows an object attached to a node — an output schema, a tool — as a 28px primary-soft pill with a 16px icon in text-secondary and the name in button-md (text). Hover lifts to primary-soft-hover; click opens the object.

The consumer provides the name and an icon; names are the object's identifier, so they may be snake_case.

Don't: truncate below 12 characters; wrap chips to a new line instead.

SegmentedControl

Edit / preview mode toggle

Open

A segmented control switches between exclusive modes: edit and preview in the header. Track is segmented-track with radius-full and 2px inset; each segment is a 32px pill; the pressed segment lifts to segmented-thumb with shadow-100 and text-emphasis, the others sit in text-secondary.

The consumer provides two to four items with an icon and an aria-label; the thumb moves with ease-move over duration-basic.

Do: use icons only in the header (labels live in tooltips). Don't: use it for more than four options or for actions — it represents state.

Forms

Input

Soft input, textarea, outline variant, invalid

Open

Inputs are soft: a 28px field (control-sm) filled with input-bg (ink at 8%), radius-md, 10px horizontal padding, input-text in text, placeholder in text-tertiary. No border at rest; focus adds a 1px input-border-focus ring, invalid a 1px input-border-invalid ring. Textareas use the same fill with radius-sm, 4px vertical and 12px left padding, 120px minimum height.

The outline variant (aui-input--outline) is transparent with a 1px primary-outline-border ring, for forms on surface rather than inside a panel.

The consumer provides the label (a field-label to the left, 60px minimum) and the value.

Do: place the label left and the field right at 28px tall inside panels. Don't: use a visible border and a fill together, or put helper text under a field — use the panel description line.

Switch

32×19 track, 13px thumb

Open

A switch is a 32×19 track (switch-track, checked switch-track-checked) with a 13px switch-thumb inset 3px, shadow-switch-thumb; the thumb slides 13px with ease-move. It is a button[role=switch] with aria-checked.

In light the checked track is near-black (gray-900); in dark it is blue-400 — keep that, it is how the source reads a checked state on dark grounds.

Do: pair it with a field-label on the left. Don't: add on/off text inside the track.

Canvas

BranchNode

A node whose outputs are named branches

Open

A branch node is a Node whose output is a list of outcomes: If/else conditions, Guardrails Pass/Fail, Classify labels. Each outcome is a 28px row on branch-bg with radius-md, right-aligned branch-label text in text-tertiary, and its own output port on the right edge of the card.

The consumer provides the branches array in evaluation order; the last row of an If/else is always Else. Rows stack with space-1 between them, under the head with space-2.

Do: keep labels short (one word or two). Use the same tint family as the plain node of that kind.
Don't: colour a branch row by outcome (Pass is not green, Fail is not red — the connector's destination says what happens). Don't put a port on the head row when branches exist.

Canvas

Ground, dot grid, connectors between ports

Open

The canvas is the canvas ground with an optional 20px dot grid in dot-grid. Nodes are absolutely positioned on it; connectors are SVG paths underneath the nodes.

Connectors are 1.5px edge strokes with horizontal tangents and both control points at the midpoint x between the two ports (AgentUI.edgePath). A selected connector is edge-selected. While dragging a new connection the line is connection-line, 1px, dashed.

Ports sit on the card edge, centred on the head row (or on each branch row). The consumer computes port positions from layout; the component draws nothing else on the canvas.

Do: keep 40px or more between a node's right edge and the next node's left edge so the curve reads. Draw connectors before nodes so cards occlude lines.
Don't: add arrowheads, labels on edges, or coloured edges per family. Don't add a minimap unless the graph exceeds the viewport.

MiniMap

Overview of a large graph with the viewport mask

Open

The minimap appears in the bottom-right corner of the canvas once the graph is larger than the viewport: a 180×110 panel card with radius-xl, nodes as family-tinted rectangles at scale, and the current viewport as an alpha-06 mask with an alpha-20 outline. Dragging the mask pans the canvas.

It never shows labels or edges — it is for orientation, and the canvas is for reading. It hides again when the whole graph fits.

Don't: show it on an empty or small graph, or let it overlap the floating toolbar (the toolbar is centred; the minimap is right-aligned, both 16px from the bottom).

Node

One node per step: tinted icon tile, name, type line, ports

Open

A node is one step on the canvas: a white card (node-surface, radius-2xl, 1px ring shadow-node) holding a 28px icon tile, the step name in node-title and its type in node-meta.

The consumer provides kind (which picks the icon and the tile tint), title and an optional meta line. Ports are drawn by the component: an input dot on the left edge, an output dot on the right, both 8px handle with a shadow-handle ring. start has no input; end has no output.

Tints by family. Core agents use tint-agent; logic steps (Classify, If/else, While, User approval) use tint-logic; Start and End use tint-end; Note uses tint-note; tools (File search, Guardrails, MCP) use tint-tool; data steps (Transform, Set state) use tint-data. The glyph inside is always node-ink. Never introduce a seventh tint — add a kind to an existing family instead.

Selection swaps the ring to shadow-node-selected; there is no fill change, no border, no glow. Hover changes nothing on the card (the cursor is grab).

Do: keep titles to one line (the card grows to fit; never wrap). Show a meta line for every kind except Note.
Don't: put buttons inside a node; the inspector holds every control. Don't colour the title by family.

Panels

Inspector

Settings panel for the selected node

Open

The inspector is a 360px panel (panel, 0.5px panel-border, radius-2xl, space-4 padding) that edits the selected node. It floats over the canvas at the right, top-aligned with the palette.

Layout, top to bottom: the head (panel-title in text, a one-line panel-description in text-tertiary, duplicate and delete ghost icon buttons at the top right); then a column of rows separated by space-3. A row is a field-label (text-emphasis, 60px minimum) on the left and its control on the right: a soft Input, an inline Select value (field-value with a 12px up/down glyph, no border — the whole row is the trigger), a Switch, or an add (+) ghost button. Long text (Instructions) stacks: label row with its actions, then a Textarea. Chips list attached objects such as a schema. A border divider precedes the footer with More (chevron) on the left and Evaluate (external link) on the right.

The consumer provides the field list; controls are the system's Input, Switch, Chip and Button.

Do: one row per setting, label left, control right, every row 28px tall. Show the description line even when obvious.
Don't: draw borders around inputs (soft input-bg fill only), or add section headings inside the panel — use the divider.

Palette

Grouped list of node kinds to drag onto the canvas

Open

The palette is a 200px panel (panel, 0.5px panel-border, radius-2xl, space-2 inner padding) listing every node kind in four groups: Core, Tools, Logic, Data — in that order.

Each group has a palette-section label in text-tertiary, then 40px rows: a 24px tinted tile, the kind's name in palette-item (text-emphasis). Rows hover to ghost-hover and press to ghost-active; they are draggable onto the canvas and also insert on click.

The consumer provides the sections and items; the family tint comes from kind. The panel floats over the canvas at page-gutter from the left edge, top-aligned with the header.

Do: keep the four groups even when a group has one item. Match the order a person builds in: Core first.
Don't: add search unless there are more than about 20 kinds. Don't show descriptions in the row — the inspector describes a kind once placed.

Chrome

Chat

Play mode: user pills, one row per step, the reply under the agent, composer with send/stop

Open

The chat is play mode's whole surface: it is how you talk to the workflow you just drew. It lives in a 480px panel on the right of the canvas (inspector-width plus room for prose) and fills a page when used on its own.

Three parts, top to bottom. A top row with a ghost New chat button in text-secondary. A scrolling log (role="log", aria-live="polite") with 12px between entries: the user's message is a primary-soft pill on the right at 16/24, at most 85% wide; every step the run touches is a row on the left — the kind's 16px glyph, the step name at 16/24 weight 500, an optional status word in text-tertiary (routed to Recipe, blocked in danger-text), the response id in mono 12px indented under it, and the model's reply in text-emphasis under that, indented 24px to align with the name. While a step runs its glyph is a 14px aui-spinner. With no entries the log shows the empty state: a 32px primary-soft tile with the wand glyph, "Preview your agent", "Prompt the agent as if you're the user."

The composer is a surface card with 24px corners and a hairline ring, pinned to the bottom with 8px around it. The textarea sits on top (16/24, grows to 160px, ↵ sends and ⇧↵ breaks the line); the bar under it has an attach button on the left and a 32px primary-solid send pill on the right. Send is disabled on empty text (alpha-12), and while the run is busy it becomes Stop: the same pill with a 10px square.

Replies are markdown rendered by the consumer and passed as an element; the aui-msg__reply styles cover paragraphs, headings, lists, inline and block code and links. Do: keep every step the run touched in the log, even silent ones — it is how people see routing happen. Don't: put the agent's reply in a bubble; only the user gets one.

CommandPalette

⌘K: add a node, jump to one, or run an action

Open

The command palette opens with ⌘K and is the fastest way to do anything in the builder. It floats at 12vh from the top on the backdrop: a 560px surface-elevated card with radius-2xl and shadow-400, a 16px search row with a search glyph and an esc key hint, a grouped list, and a footer with the key hints.

Groups, in order: Add node (every palette kind, with its tile), Jump to (nodes on the canvas by name, with their tile), Actions (Preview, Monitor, Evaluate, Code, Tidy layout, Toggle theme, New workflow — each with its shortcut as a kbd hint). Typing filters across all groups by label and keywords; a group with no matches disappears; no matches at all shows "No matches". ↑↓ move, ↵ selects, esc closes, as does a click on the backdrop. Rows are 40px, the selected row is ghost-hover.

The consumer provides the groups; each item carries a kind (shows the node tile) or an icon, an optional hint, and an onSelect.

Do: keep Add node first — it is what people reach for most. Don't: nest groups, or put destructive actions in the palette.

Header

Builder header: back, title, Draft badge, mode toggle, actions

Open

The builder header runs across the top of the canvas on the canvas ground (no bar, no border): a back ghost button, the workflow name in page-title (36px/590, +0.36px tracking), the Draft badge, the edit/preview segmented control centred, and at the right an actions (…) ghost pill and the solid Code pill.

The consumer provides the name, the state, and the right-hand actions.

Do: keep the name editable in place. Don't: add a breadcrumb or a logo — the back arrow is the only navigation.

ThemeToggle

Light and dark, remembered per browser

Open

The theme toggle is a ghost pill icon button that flips data-theme on <html> between light and dark and remembers the choice in localStorage (agent-ui.theme). It shows a sun in light and a moon in dark, and sits in the header's right-hand group, before the Evaluate button, and in the page header on the workflows home.

Every token carries a dark value, so nothing else changes; the design system's own dark values (derived tints, alpha inks for charts) are what the toggle reveals.

Don't: add a third "system" option in the control itself — follow the OS setting on first load, then let the toggle override it.

Toolbar

Floating canvas toolbar: pan, select, undo, redo

Open

The floating toolbar sits bottom-centre over the canvas: a 56px pill (toolbar-bg, shadow-toolbar, space-2 padding, space-2 gaps) of 40px round ghost icon buttons. The active mode is pressed (primary-soft fill); unavailable history actions are disabled in text-tertiary.

The consumer provides the item list; order is pan, select, then history.

Don't: put labelled buttons here, or more than six items.

Data display

BarChart

Runs per day stacked by verdict, with hover tooltip

Open

The bar chart is for counts over time. Bars are thin (at most 28px, 60% of the step), the top segment has a 4px rounded data-end, stacked segments are separated by a 2px gap of the ground, the grid is three recessive border lines with 11px text-tertiary ticks, and the x-axis labels every day up to 14 and every fifth beyond. Hovering a column shows a tooltip with the day's total and each segment.

Colour follows the status palette and only there: green-500 pass, red-500 fail, gray-250 ungraded. A single-series chart is all gray-500. The consumer provides days (label and segments) and keys (segment key, legend label, class); the legend in the card head is mandatory for two or more series.

Do: keep the zero baseline; sort time left to right. Don't: add a second y-axis, draw a value on every bar, or use a hue for a series that is not a status.

Bars

Magnitude by category: one hue, direct values

Open

Horizontal bars rank categories by one measure. Each row is a label (with the node's 22px tile when the category is a node), a 6px gray-100 track with a gray-500 fill scaled to the maximum, and the value right-aligned in 12px text-secondary tabular numerals. Rows are sorted by value, largest first, and capped at eight.

The consumer provides rows with a label, a numeric value for scale and a display string for the number as it should read (1.08 s, not 1080).

Do: show the unit in the display string. Don't: colour bars by category — the label carries identity, the bar carries magnitude.

Card

Container for one chart or table: title, subtitle, legend, body, footer

Open

A card is the unit of a dashboard: panel fill, 0.5px panel-border, radius-2xl, space-5 padding. Its head holds a 14px/600 title and a 12px text-tertiary subtitle on the left and, when the body has two or more series, a legend on the right. The body is exactly one chart or table. An optional footer sits under a border-subtle rule for the time window, a count, or a "View all" link.

The consumer provides the title, the subtitle (say what the number means, not what the chart is), the body element and, when it applies, the legend pairs.

Do: one question per card; name the metric and its window in the subtitle. Don't: nest cards, put controls in the head (they belong in the page header), or stack two charts in one card.

DataTable

Recent runs with verdict badges

Open

The data table lists records: 13px rows, 12px text-tertiary headers over a border rule, border-subtle rules between rows, numeric columns right-aligned with tabular numerals, long text truncated at 280px. Clickable rows (aui-table--rows) hover to ghost-hover with rounded ends.

Status goes in a badge with an icon and a word (✓ Pass, × Fail, Ungraded), never colour alone. Timestamps are text-secondary; identifiers are aui-table__mono.

The consumer provides the columns (key, label, numeric flag, class) and rows whose cells are strings or elements.

Do: newest first for runs; keep to six columns and link to the detail view for more. Don't: zebra-stripe, or add vertical rules.

Distribution

Where each branch node sent the run

Open

A distribution shows how one whole splits: for a branch node, how many runs went down each branch. Each group is the node's name with its tile, one 10px stacked bar whose segments are separated by 2px of ground and shaded from gray-500 down in the order given, and a key beneath listing each part with its count in bold.

Segments are greys because branches have no fixed identity colour; the key, not the shade, names them. Order parts largest first so the darkest segment is the most common path.

Do: cap at six parts and fold the rest into "Other". Don't: use pass/fail colours here even for Guardrails — the key already says Pass and Fail.

EmptyState

Nothing to show yet, with the one action that fixes it

Open

An empty state is centred in the space the content would fill: a 32px primary-soft tile with an 18px glyph, a 16px/600 title in text-emphasis, one 14px line in text-secondary saying what will fill it, and at most one solid pill button that does so.

Do: name the action that produces data. Don't: use an illustration, or more than one sentence.

Menu

Actions menu with a danger item after a separator

Open

A menu floats from an icon button: surface-elevated fill, radius-xl, shadow-overlay plus shadow-hairline, 6px padding, items 32px tall with a 14px glyph in text-secondary and a 14px label. A separator is a border hairline bleeding to the edges. Destructive actions are danger-text, always last, always after a separator.

The consumer provides the items; the menu closes on outside click and Escape. Don't: put more than six items in one menu, or two danger items.

PageHeader

Title, subtitle, filter and primary action for a page

Open

The page header opens every full-page view (the workflows home, Monitor): a 28px heading title in text-emphasis, one subtitle line in text-tertiary that states the counts the page is about, and an actions group on the right holding a text segmented control (filters or a time range) and at most one solid pill button.

The consumer provides the title, the subtitle and the actions. Below it, content sits on the aui-page scaffold (1200px max width, space-8 side padding, space-10 top) and in aui-grid rows with space-4 gaps.

Do: put the time range or filter here, never inside the cards. Don't: add breadcrumbs, a search box, or a second primary button.

StatTile

Headline number with sparkline and delta

Open

A stat tile is the headline number for one metric: a 12px label with a 14px glyph in text-tertiary, the value at 32px/600 with tabular numerals (a unit at 16px in text-tertiary beside it), a 112×32 sparkline on the right, and a footer with a delta pill and the comparison it refers to.

The sparkline is one hue (gray-400 line, alpha-05 area) with a dot on the last point; it shows shape, never values. The delta pill is success-surface when the change is good for the metric and danger-surface when bad — the consumer decides which direction is good (lower latency is good). No prior period: pass delta: null and a foot that explains the number.

Do: four tiles across at most; keep labels to two words; let latency tiles show the percentile in the label. Don't: colour the value itself, or use a sparkline when there is one data point (pass series: null).

WorkflowCard

One workflow on the home page: graph thumbnail, status, stats

Open

A workflow card is the entry to one workflow: a 132px thumbnail of its graph on the dotted canvas ground (nodes as white cards with family-tinted tiles, edges as hairlines), then the name at 16px/600 beside a Draft or Live badge, a meta line, and three stats under a border-subtle rule — runs in the last 7 days, grader pass rate, last run.

The whole card is the link; the … button at the top right opens a Menu with Open, Duplicate, Publish/Unpublish and Delete (danger, last, after a separator). Hover lifts the card with shadow-300.

The consumer provides the graph (node positions and kinds, edges), the status, the meta line and the stats. Don't: render the thumbnail from a screenshot, or show more than three stats.

Pages

Dashboard

Monitor page assembled from the data-display components

Open

The Monitor page is the reference assembly of the data-display components: a PageHeader with the time range, four StatTiles, then a 3:2 row (BarChart card, Bars card) and a 2:3 row (Distribution card, DataTable card), all in aui-grid rows with space-4 gaps on the canvas ground.

Reading order is the hierarchy: the headline numbers first, the trend second, the breakdowns third, the records last. Every number on the page comes from the same trace records, so a change in one tile is visible in the chart below it.

Build any new dashboard from these parts in this order; add a card only when it answers a question the existing ones do not.

Data display

Dashboards in Agent-UI answer one question per card and keep the chrome as quiet as the canvas. The components are PageHeader, Card, StatTile, BarChart, Bars, Distribution, DataTable, WorkflowCard, Menu and EmptyState; the Dashboard page shows them assembled.

Form follows the question

Use a StatTile for a single headline number with its trend; a BarChart for counts over time; Bars for magnitude across a few categories; a Distribution when a whole splits into parts; a DataTable for records someone will scan or open. If the number is one value with no history, use the tile alone with no sparkline. Never draw a chart to show one number.

Colour does one job at a time

Magnitude is one ink: alpha-50 fills on alpha-06 tracks, an alpha-40 sparkline over an alpha-05 area — alpha tokens so the marks keep their weight in dark. Status is the only other colour on a dashboard and it is reserved: green-500 pass and red-500 fail in bars, success-surface/danger-surface in badges and delta pills, always beside a word or an icon, never alone. Node identity on a chart comes from the node's family tile next to its label, not from the bar. Nothing on a dashboard uses info-*, discovery-* or the hue scales directly.

Marks are thin and grids recede

Bars are at most 28px wide with a 4px rounded data-end on the top segment and 2px of ground between stacked segments. Lines are 1.5px. Grid lines use border; axes use border-strong; ticks are 11px text-tertiary. Values are direct labels in text-secondary tabular numerals, placed selectively (on hover, on the ends of Bars), never on every mark.

Text wears text tokens

Titles, values, labels and legends stay in text-emphasis, text, text-secondary and text-tertiary; a coloured swatch beside them carries identity. Numbers use font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums so columns and tiles line up.

Layout

A page is aui-page (1200px max, space-8 sides) with a PageHeader, then aui-stack rows of aui-grid at space-4 gaps: tiles four across (aui-grid--tiles), then 3:2 and 2:3 rows (aui-grid--3-2, aui-grid--2-3) that collapse to one column under 960px. Controls for time range and filters live in the PageHeader, never inside a card. An EmptyState takes the place of the whole grid when there is nothing to show, and names the action that produces data.

Interaction

Every chart with a plot has a hover layer: a tooltip on BarChart columns, a title on Bars rows and Distribution segments. Table rows that open something are aui-table--rows and hover to ghost-hover. Cards do not hover; WorkflowCards do, with shadow-300.
